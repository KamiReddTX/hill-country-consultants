          How to

          Put a new issue on the site

          Issues → fill in the month, number, title and who is on the cover, choose the cover image, Save. The newest issue that has reached its online date (the 8th) shows on the home page; before then it shows as "Coming next".

          Let subscribers read an issue

          Page scans → choose the issue, choose the PDF, Upload. Keep this tab open until it says every page is done. Only readers whose level includes the site can open them.

          Add an old story to the archive

          Articles → headline, the date it ran, byline and section, and a link if it has a page. It is filed under its month automatically. Every story opens to any account a year after it ran.

          Give someone a subscription by hand

          Members → find her, Change, pick the level and the date it ends, Save. She sees it the next time she opens a page.

          Something looks wrong

          Nothing here can break the design of the site. If a save fails, the message says why. Write to the web team with the page and what you did.
