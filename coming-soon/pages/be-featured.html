Be featured

A Black woman business owner: what she built, what it cost her, and how the numbers actually look. If that is you, or a woman you know, tell us about the business. Put yourself forward; you do not have to wait to be found.

How it works

  1. Tell us about the business below. It takes about ten minutes.
  2. An editor reads every submission and checks the business is real and running.
  3. If it is a fit for an upcoming issue, she is contacted for an interview and photographs.
  4. She sees nothing printed about her without her own permission.

Being featured is editorial: it is never sold, and a feature cannot be bought. If you would like to promote your business, that is advertising, and it is kept apart from the stories.
