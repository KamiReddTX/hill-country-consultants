Shout-outs · in every issue

Her Flowers

We’re handing out flowers while she can still smell them. Who won it, who opened it, who got confirmed, who broke the record, who bought the building, who walked that stage. You nominate her, and we name her here. That’s ten to twelve women every month, starting in January.

What gets printed

  • Her name
  • What she did
  • Where and when
  • The source it was confirmed against

If it cannot be confirmed, the desk writes and asks you for help rather than print it unsourced. Bigger than an entry? If she is doing the work week after week in her own neighborhood, nominate her for She Showed Up instead. That one runs at length.

Send someone her flowers

Somebody you know deserves hers. Tell us what she did, when it happened, and where it can be confirmed. Entries print named, dated, and sourced.

You’ve got 800 characters, and there’s no wrong way to use them. Write it the way it sounds coming out of your mouth. What you send is what we print, word for word.
