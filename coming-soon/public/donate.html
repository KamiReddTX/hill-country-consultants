Reader-funded

Support the newsroom

Records requests cost money. So does confirming a case with an agency, mailing an issue, and paying for the photography that runs. Reader money is what keeps this reporting free of anyone who would rather it did not exist.

Make a gift

Give once, or every month. You choose the amount.

Online giving opens shortly. To give now, mail a check payable to Dynamic Black Women Media, Inc. to Atlanta, Georgia, or write to info@dbw-news.com and we will send you a secure link.

No goods or services are provided in exchange for this donation. Dynamic Black Women Media, Inc. (formerly Dear Black Woman Media, Inc.) is a 501(c)(3) public charity under 509(a)(2); your gift is tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. EIN 33-3726585. You receive an emailed receipt, and a written acknowledgment for gifts of $250 or more.

Card payments are processed by Stripe; DBW never sees or stores your card number. Donor information is never sold, rented, or traded, and you may remain anonymous. Gifts are put to work as they arrive and are generally not refundable; if you gave in error or twice, write to us within 30 days and it is refunded. A monthly gift can be stopped at any time.

Where it goes

Records and filings

Public records requests, court documents, and the data bought to check a number.

Photography

Commissioned and licensed images, with photographers paid and credited.

Printing and postage

Getting the issue printed and into the mailbox of every subscriber.

The podcast

Recording, editing and distributing the show, free to listen.

Other ways to give

  • By check: payable to Dynamic Black Women Media, Inc., mailed to Atlanta, Georgia. Include your email for the receipt.
  • Employer matching gifts: many employers match a gift. Ask your HR team, and give them our EIN, 33-3726585.
  • Donor-advised funds: recommend a grant to Dynamic Black Women Media, Inc., EIN 33-3726585.
  • Sponsor an issue, or advertise: businesses can advertise with DBW. Advertising and gifts are kept separate, with separate receipts.

If your gift is larger

A gift of $2,520 a year names you, or your business, in the supporters list printed in the magazine, with a line about what you do if you want one. Being named is recognition rather than something you bought, so your whole gift stays deductible. Anonymity is available at any amount and costs you nothing. To talk about a larger gift, write to info@dbw-news.com.

Who is asking

Dynamic Black Women Media, Inc. is a charitable organization at Atlanta, Georgia. A full and fair description of our programs, and a financial statement, are available on request from that address, from info@dbw-news.com, or on (470) 236-1755. We use no paid solicitor: every appeal comes from the newsroom, and registration with a state is not that state's endorsement of us. Nothing here is tax advice; ask your accountant.
