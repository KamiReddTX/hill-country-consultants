Advertise, or give

Dynamic Black Women Media, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit newsroom, and a business can put money into it two ways: buy advertising, which is what this page is for, or make a gift, which is deductible as a charitable contribution. They are separate payments with separate receipts, and we do not blend them.

Advertising

You sell

Offers, prices, discount codes and a call to action, within the law. It is labelled as an advertisement and kept clearly apart from editorial. A commercial purchase rather than a gift: generally deductible to you as a business expense, and your receipt says exactly that.

A gift

You are thanked

A gift of $2,520 a year puts your business in the printed supporters list, with a line about what you do. No offer, no price, no call to action: recognition rather than a placement, so the whole gift is deductible. Ask us for every tier and the exact receipt wording.

Nothing here is tax advice: ask your accountant. Our determination letter and W-9 are yours on request.
