Community hero · one per issue

She Showed Up

Every issue features one community hero, nominated by a reader. A Black woman making a difference where she lives: an organizer, a leader, or a woman who simply keeps showing up. Nominations are open year round.

What happens next

  1. Your nomination is read.
  2. Who she is and what she does is verified.
  3. She is contacted for an interview, and for her own permission.
  4. If she runs, you hear first.

One hero runs per issue, so a strong nomination may wait a month or two rather than be turned down. Writing a strong nomination: name what she actually does, week to week. Give one story, not a list of adjectives. Tell us what it costs her. Say who else would speak about her.

Is it a one-time win? If she won something, opened something or crossed a stage, send it to Her Flowers instead.

Her permission, and yours

You are nominating another woman, so her contact details are needed above. The desk writes to every nominee for her own written permission before a word about her is printed. A nomination alone is not consent.

Submitting a nomination does not guarantee publication, and it may be edited for length, clarity, and accuracy. Every nomination is verified, and the nominee is contacted for her own release before publication. If she declines, it does not run. Your permission covers your own words and materials only. Write to us at any time to withdraw a nomination that has not yet run.
